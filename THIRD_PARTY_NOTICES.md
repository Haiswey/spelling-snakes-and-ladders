# Third-party notices

## Illustrations
All pictures (animals, food, objects, numbers, avatars, icons) were generated with Canva's AI image generator as kawaii-style sticker sheets, cut into single transparent images by `src/cut_sheets.py`, and embedded in the game. Use is subject to Canva's terms.
Three small interface icons (speaker, mute, tick) are hand-drawn vectors.

## Voice

## Word list
Vocabulary taken from the Super Minds 1 course map (Cambridge University Press). Only single words and short original sentences are used.
