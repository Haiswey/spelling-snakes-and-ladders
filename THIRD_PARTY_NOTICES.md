# Third-party notices

## Illustrations
All pictures (animals, food, objects, numbers, avatars, icons) were generated with Canva's AI image generator as kawaii-style sticker sheets, cut into single transparent images by `src/cut_sheets.py`, and embedded in the game. Use is subject to Canva's terms.
Three small interface icons (speaker, mute, tick) are hand-drawn vectors.

## 3D dice

## Fonts
- **Azim** (Medium and Bold), a rounded school-style font with the Malaysian letter shapes, by Zainuddin Jamil (2003). A Latin subset is embedded. The font files carry no licence text (their embedding flag allows embedding); permission from the author has not been confirmed. The original files are kept outside the public repository.

## Voice
Spoken audio generated with Microsoft Edge text-to-speech (voice en-GB-SoniaNeural) using the `edge-tts` package.

## Word list
Vocabulary taken from the Super Minds 1 course map (Cambridge University Press). Only single words and short original sentences are used.
