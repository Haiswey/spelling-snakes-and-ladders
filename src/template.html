How to play

  1. Roll the dice. Tap the dice or the orange button.
  2. Spell the word. Listen, then type it or tap the letters.
  3. Spell it right and climb the ladder.
  4. Spell it wrong on a snake and slide down.
  5. First to square 36 wins!
